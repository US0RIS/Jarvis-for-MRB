# Five-Year Friction Inventory → Human Extension Work Program

This is the explicit tracking contract for the ordinary-life experiment spanning
ages 25–30. **A row is not a claim that Jarvis can already complete every
example.** Items are separated into:
- **Core**: generic infrastructure implemented in `life_fabric.py` and the Life
  screen, when the user explicitly enrolls a record or logs an inconvenience.
- **Existing separate**: functionality previously implemented behind its own
  permissions/setup (Gmail/Calendar, Reality Graph, Mission Control, home-light
  control, Reality Lens, private paired-device screens, consented meeting notes).
- **Integration required**: an actual provider, access grant, device, authenticated
  evidence, or hardware is needed before any autonomous workflow can work.
- **Research/hardware**: a new machine or substantial new engineering, not a
  pretend software switch.

No stage treats reading this inventory as authority to observe, spend, disclose,
contact a person, change medical care, administer household locks, or control a
physical device. Neither a task entry nor a source mention proves execution.

## The twelve superpower directions

| Extension | Current executable piece | Missing end-to-end ability |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Black Box | Existing opt-in world events, Reality Lens 30-day snapshot memory, manual handoff packets | Uniform historical recall across user-consented devices and files; user-directed retention/erasure across separate data stores |
| Background Watchers | Existing time-limited explicit external watches and Goal Guardian | Authorized per-domain provider observations with user-configurable watches and actual OS notification delivery |
| Reality Mesh / Lens | Exact named-place lookups, supported public sources and light haptic comparison | Universal remote coverage or automatic visual memory; provider and geographic constraints remain |
| Presence | Explicit temporary private remote screenshots and exact benign app launches | Physical robot gripper/camera with manual low-latency control and independent object-handling verification |
| Parallel Workspaces | Existing Agent/Conductor limited tasks; Life handoff restore packet | Isolated concurrent work sessions, resource limits, conflict resolution and status reconciliation |
| Personal Simulation | Bounded user-supplied free-time arithmetic | Verified calendar/finance/transport models and uncertainty-aware conditional scenarios |
| Temporal Intelligence | Life due-window classifier + dependencies; existing Calendar and Guardian independently | Calibrated multi-source deadlines, per-source freshness, and background consequences |
| Certainty Engine | Source-classified Life receipts + Reality Graph observations | Provider-specific verifiers for each actual outcome; never assume provider acceptance equals personal receipt |
| Personal Resource Model | Explicit manually enrolled assets and tasks | Inventory sensors, merchant exports, warranty systems, subscription and vehicle APIs |
| Opportunity Discovery | Due windows, reviewable repeated-friction candidates, existing separate research | User-authorized discovery across relevant official/provider sources; no inferred eligibility |
| Friction Observatory | Explicit log; three distinct days within 30 days raises review candidate | Consented, privacy-preserving event collectors and independently evaluated permanent fixes |
| Universal Handoff | Explicit context, next step, linked task packet across iPhone and private backend | Rehydrating exact live state across arbitrary external applications and devices |

## Full life inventory and implementation boundaries

Each grouped line represents multiple concrete desired outcomes. In the core
ledger you can record the underlying task/asset and its deadline, preserve a
handoff, attach a self-confirmation, and identify recurrence. **The complete
automated resolution named in a line requires the indicated integrations.**

### 1. Morning readiness
- Sleep-window and environment-based waking; temperature at waking; charge
  phone, headphones and watch; identify tomorrow's laptop/gym/travel equipment:
  **integration required** (consented wearables, charging/plug telemetry,
  climate controls and a specific readiness policy).
- Leave at correct time, context-sensitive clothing, umbrella, known schedule,
  route disruption and departure inventory: **existing separate** Calendar,
  selected weather, MapKit and Guardian primitives; **integration required**
  wardrobe inventory, live contextual departure checks and accepted notifications.
- Milk/coffee/eggs/toothpaste running out, breakfast gap, washed clothes, keys,
  wallet, medication taken or not: **core** explicit assets/tasks; **integration
  required** stock sensors, tags, health-specific logging and honest freshness.
- Lights and locks: **existing separate** exact enrolled HomeKit light actions;
  **integration required** separately authorized lock and scene permissions
  and real readback.

### 2. Journey and errands
- Current departure, route change, late ETA, transit platform, missed connection,
  early-arrival waiting place, efficient multi-stop errands: **existing separate**
  maps/calendar/Guardian pieces; **integration required** actual live provider,
  location consent and explicit message-sending scope.
- Parking location, street cleaning, meter, garage, charger/fuel, charging
  availability and parking validity: **core** tasks/assets; **integration
  required** vehicle and parking-provider records and exact sensor readback.
- Package drop-off, returning item when passing carrier, buying needed item
  when passing shop: **core** explicit obligations; **integration required**
  foreground geofences, business hours and consented inventory.

### 3. Work continuity
- Yesterday's workspace, prior decision rationale, searching files/attachments,
  versions and approvals, meeting preparation, follow-up status, meeting links,
  task owner, meeting decisions, interruption recovery, weekly finished-work
  history, waiting on a colleague, long-running job completion: **existing
  separate** portions in Gmail/Calendar/world model/meeting notes/Conductor;
  **core** handoff/task/receipt continuity; **integration required**
  cross-app sessions, authoritative project systems and verified work outcomes.
- Form filling, scheduling, sending email and operating a workstation:
  **existing separate** narrowly permissioned routes; broader workflows need
  source-specific write authorization and independent readback, not model
  improvisation.

### 4. Food and groceries
- Meal decisions, preferences, pantry, duplicate purchases, grocery lists,
  shelf life, perishables, leftover use, serving sizes, cooking ingredient
  shortfalls and timers: **core** explicit assets/tasks; **integration
  required** permitted shopping imports, inventory capture, appliance
  telemetry and food-handling evidence.
- Delivery ETA, delivery conflicting with calendar, recipe-guided reminders,
  restaurant discovery near a route and bill splitting: **existing separate**
  mission graph can process normalized delivery evidence; **integration
  required** actual order-provider accounts/receipts and transaction controls.

### 5. Home nervous system
- Trash schedule, bins, mail, laundry washer/dryer cycles, filters, watering
  plants, appliances, device battery, router, leaks, smoke-detector chirps,
  fridge temperature and equipment maintenance: **core** explicit task and
  inventory ledger; **integration required** actual utility/sensor/device
  endpoints and supported safe interventions.
- Package presence, permitted HomeKit lights and selected public observations:
  **existing separate** bounded relevant primitives; do not claim general
  appliance/lock/garage control or successful physical service without evidence.

### 6. Shopping and procurement
- Compare specs and compatibility, price windows, coupon and card benefits,
  replacement timing, stock, trial cancellation, unused or duplicate services,
  returns, return labels, refunds, gift tracking, warranty and receipts,
  resale/donation: **core** purchase transition checklist, asset ledger,
  deadlines, receipts and resource lifecycle; **integration required** merchant,
  issuer and provider accounts, live offers, legitimate checkout and scoped
  payments. No purchase authority inherited from Life Fabric.

### 7. Moving and transitions
- Lease/key dates, utility transfer, address changes, movers/elevator/loading
  zone, condition photography, furniture/door measurements, box inventory,
  first-night items, internet installation, final bills and deposit return:
  **core** eight-task Moving template and optional dependent tasks;
  **integration required** actual landlord/utility/building/provider accounts,
  spatial capture and money/contract permissions.
- New job, trip, purchase, vehicle maintenance and clinician follow-up:
  **core** five additional explicit templates; all external steps remain
  separately authorized.

### 8. Money and bureaucracy
- Bills, payment deduplication, unexpected charges, annual fees, expenses and
  reimbursements, tax documents, refunds, insurance renewals/claims,
  cancellation/notice windows, identity documents, repetitive forms and
  support calls: **core** exact obligations, source-classified receipts,
  deadlines and handoff; **integration required** banks, statements,
  tax/insurer APIs, identity vault and permitted action routes.
- Medical and financial decisions cannot be delegated merely by enrolling an
  obligation or receiving a public source value.

### 9. Relationships
- Birthdays, suitable gifts, saved preferences, replying and follow-up,
  making plans, family visits, group restaurant selection, invitations,
  bringing items, contact history, voluntarily shared photos and
  staying in touch: **core** manually entered intentions/tasks and handoffs;
  **existing separate** private user-provided notecard and calendar pieces;
  **integration required** consentful contacts/photos and per-recipient
  sharing/sending approval. No stranger face identification.

### 10. Health continuity
- Appointments, clinician questions, referrals, networks, form repetition,
  prescriptions, refill pickup, symptom chronology, labs, recommended
  follow-ups, dentist, contacts and insurance appeals: **core** Health follow-up
  checklist and self-reported task status; **integration required** supported
  medical records/pharmacy/insurer consent and independently sourced claims.
- Workouts, recovery, sleeping environment: **existing separate** user-enrolled
  wearable/environment primitives; **integration required** validated
  interpretation. No diagnosis, prescriptions or treatment changes inferred.

### 11. Digital life
- Password/passkey retrieval, login email, duplicate account discovery,
  downloads, storage, backups and actual restore tests, file versions,
  ongoing jobs, cross-device handoff, bookmarks and rationale, app interface
  changes, unwanted calls, voicemail, security alerts, crashes and
  disconnected peripherals: **existing separate** exact device fabric,
  local world model and narrow tools; **core** handoffs/records/receipts;
  **integration required** account-approved OS/browser/password-manager,
  cloud-backup and diagnostics capabilities. Do not store password copies
  in Life Fabric.

### 12. Travel operating system
- Booking comparisons, airport/terminal, tight connection, seat,
  passport/visa validity, packing, gate/flight changes, downstream
  rescheduling, rental counter, airport parking, offline documents,
  language translation, expenses, delayed bags, hotel checkout and
  groceries on return: **core** travel checklist, deadlines, dependencies
  and handoff; **existing separate** selected calendar/maps/public airspace;
  **integration required** authoritative ticket/carrier/baggage/hotel
  providers, user permission, and physical phone acceptance.

### 13. Vehicle and asset stewardship
- Service, vehicle warnings, tires, slow pressure loss, registration,
  coverage, repair estimate, recall, service completion, pickup, permit,
  transport while serviced, washing: **core** vehicle transition and
  assets; **integration required** exact vehicle data, manufacturer
  maintenance documentation, suitable telemetry and verified provider
  state. No automatic approval of repair or physical control.

### 14. Recovery engine
- Router/power failure, leak, misplaced wallet/phone, compromised card,
  lockout, fridge, canceled delivery, no-show, device failure,
  missed appointment, out-of-stock substitution: **core** pending
  obligation, explicit outcome-unknown receipt, next step and new
  handoff; **existing separate** selected environmental/device evidence;
  **integration required** verified incidents, bounded plan alternatives,
  external service accounts and approvals. Never silently retry payment
  or a physical operation after an ambiguous response.

### 15. Accumulation and maintenance
- Dormant accounts, subscriptions, emergency contacts, address mismatches,
  old possessions, duplicated files, obsolete reminders, abandoned goals,
  lost documents, old project reasoning, outdated procedures:
  **core** editable, retire-able, deletable life records, manual inventory,
  explicit handoff and their provenance; **integration required** genuine
  cross-account discovery and per-source retention/erasure.

### 16. Unnecessary uncertainty
- Did I lock/close/pay/send/cancel/submit/receive/backup/repair/arrive?
  **core** separate unknown, self-confirmed, provider-reported,
  sensor-observed and document-supported completion states;
  **existing separate** Conductor's readback and Reality Graph's
  freshness/evidence distinction; **integration required** actual endpoint-
  specific verification before claiming the outside world changed.

### 17. Invisible problems and opportunities
- Quiet renewals, claim/benefit/refund deadlines, warranty expiry,
  security exposure, battery degradation, leak warnings, overcommitment,
  recurring failure, missed opportunities, drift from a personal goal:
  **core** deadline and dependency readiness, explicit friction candidates,
  illustrative time what-if and goal/asset entries; **existing separate**
  consented watches and Goal Guardian; **integration required** approved
  provider discovery, local consent, uncertainty models and effective
  interventions.

## Release reality

**Available in the Life Fabric code and iPhone Life screen:** manual
create/list/retire/delete; explicit user completion; source-classified
backend receipts; timezone-aware seven-day readiness; explicit inventory;
six idempotent life-transition checklists; explicit private handoff
packets; friction candidate detection after three distinct UTC days in 30;
user-supplied time-budget arithmetic; manual friction-history deletion.

**Not yet end to end:** automatic handling of every grouped scenario above.
The installed sources and permissions in the real user's devices, accounts
and geography determine which external steps are actually possible. The
backend exposes zero new external action authority. No device or provider
may be claimed connected on the basis of a catalog entry, template, or
synthetic test. Physical-device acceptance is separate from iOS simulator CI.
